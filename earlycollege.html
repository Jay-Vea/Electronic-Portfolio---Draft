EARLY COLLEGE

Early College has been part of my life since before the start of my Freshmen Year at Waipahu High School. It is a program for low income, First generation College, or underrepresented students to get ahead in their academic careers. They do this by offering classes from colleges around Hawai'i, allowing you to earn credits for your associates, or potentially graduating with it along side your diploma.

EARLY COLLEGE CLASSES TAKEN

IS 103 (Introduction to College) HIST 151 (History of the World) GEO 102 (Geography) LSK 110 (Learning Skills) SP 151 (Speech) ART 112 (Digital Art) HWST 281 (Hawaiian Studies) ENG 100 (Writing intensive) ICS 111 (Intro to Comp Sci) MATH 103 (Algebra 2) BOT 130 (Botany Studies) BOT 130L (Botany labs) HSER 100 (Self Exploration) HWST 107 (Hawaiian Studies) SP 251 (Speech) THEA 221 (Theater) ECON 130 (Microeconomics)
